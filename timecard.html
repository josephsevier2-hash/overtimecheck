How to use the time card calculator

Enter the time you started and finished work each day. If you took an unpaid break, enter the number of minutes you were not working.

The calculator will subtract your unpaid breaks and calculate your total weekly hours.

This calculator is intended as a general estimate. Actual payroll calculations may depend on your employer's timekeeping and applicable employment rules.
