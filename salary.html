How does hourly pay convert to salary?

To estimate annual earnings from an hourly wage, multiply the hourly rate by the number of hours worked per week and then by the number of weeks worked per year.

For example, someone earning $20 per hour and working 40 hours per week for 52 weeks would have estimated gross annual earnings of $41,600.

This is an estimate of gross earnings before taxes, benefits, deductions, overtime, or other adjustments.
