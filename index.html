How does overtime pay work?

Overtime pay is generally calculated by multiplying an employee's regular hourly rate by an applicable overtime multiplier and then multiplying that rate by the number of overtime hours worked.

This calculator provides an estimate and does not calculate taxes or deductions. Actual overtime requirements and pay rules can vary depending on applicable federal, state, local, and employment rules.
