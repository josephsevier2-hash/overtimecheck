How does overtime pay work?

Overtime pay is generally calculated by multiplying an employee's regular hourly rate by an overtime multiplier and then multiplying that rate by the number of overtime hours worked.

This calculator provides a simple estimate. Actual overtime rules can vary depending on applicable federal, state, local, and employment rules.
